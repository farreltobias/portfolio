# Contact

Scope: the Contact surface (`src/pages/contact.astro`), reached from both "Let's talk" controls on Home. Inherits the Home world (`src/styles/home.css`): bone field, hairlines, 11-step ramp, condensed caps, tracked caps, tabular mono, black-bar control with hatched chip. Magenta stays reserved for measured deltas and focus; it is not used here except as the focus ring.

Visitor mode: **Operate** inside a Persuade site — the visitor arrived decided; success is reaching Farrel through the path they prefer in one action.

Audience: the same evaluators as Home, now past the decision. Job: pick a path and use it. Content: the paths in `src/lib/contact.ts` (single source, shared with Home's closing band), Status/Location from Home's spec, "Open to Product Engineering roles and contract work." Nothing else may be claimed (no response times, languages or time zone until Farrel supplies them).

Constraints: no contact form (backend undecided); direct links only; external links open in a new tab, mailto does not; Astro-first, client JS only for the copy-address interaction; must hold for `/pt` string lengths; mobile keeps Home's stacked title-block rows, which the user approved.
