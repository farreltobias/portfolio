# About

Scope: the About page (`src/pages/about.astro`, PORT-010). It inherits the Home world (`src/styles/home.css`) and the Work page head (`src/styles/work.css`).

Visitor mode: **Read**. The visitor wants professional context beyond any one project and finishes understanding who Farrel is, how he works, and how the work grew.

Audience/job: engineering and product leaders, founders and clients who have seen or will see the case studies. The page frames that evidence and never repeats it: each claim about approach points at the sheet that shows it.

Content: verified facts only, from Farrel's CV and his 2026-10-20 career summary. Current Product Engineering work leads. Earlier client and implementation work comes after it and takes less space. No technology list, education, awards, years-of-experience count or unattributed metrics.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The drawing set's cover sheet, the one that explains how the other sheets were made. It refuses the conventional About page: portrait, bio paragraph, skills cloud and timeline of logos.

OWN-WORLD: the Home field, hairlines, condensed caps, tracked labels, mono prose, the spec block, sheet bands with zone labels, ruled rows and the closing. No magenta: the page records no before → after figure.

STORY: the visitor reads the thesis, then four working habits, each with its strongest evidence (a sheet or a measured result). Then three phases, newest first, beside a career spine (one bar per phase, the current phase filled and open at the top), ending on "See all work". They leave toward Work or Contact through an About-specific closing.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Masthead with About current; it already names the role. On the left, the zone label "Profile". In the centre, the About-only thesis in display caps, "I decide what gets built, then build it.", with a lede on how the work starts before the ticket and is judged by its effect. On the right, a spec block: In software since, Base, Languages, Open to.

FORM: Zone Sheets (inherited). A task-directed extension, so no seed roll. The one new component is the career spine, one bar per phase down the left of the rows, with year ticks and a key. It is not drawn to one scale, by Farrel's choice, so rows keep their natural height. It shows data, not decoration.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished. The page ends with an inspection at desktop and mobile widths, DESIGN.md updated for the scale bar, and no client JS.
