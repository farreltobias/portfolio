# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- Engineering leaders evaluating Farrel for Product Engineering roles.
- Product leaders evaluating technical and product judgment.
- Founders and companies looking for freelance or contract Product Engineering work.
- Potential Farrel.tech clients evaluating previous work.

Visitors arrive without prior context, usually while comparing several candidates or vendors, and need to decide
relevance quickly.

## Product Purpose

- Present Farrel's engineering work, product judgment, and measurable impact in one public destination.
- Replace résumé-led or conversation-led explanation with work-led evidence.
- Success: a visitor can judge whether Farrel is relevant to their problem or opportunity, and reach a contact path.

### Core Loop

- Visitor understands Farrel's positioning.
- Visitor explores selected work.
- Visitor evaluates a case study.
- Visitor decides whether Farrel is relevant.
- Visitor reaches a contact path.

## Positioning

- Farrel owns product decisions end to end: scoping, deciding, and shipping without a product manager translating
  requirements into tickets.
- Technical and architectural choices are framed by business impact — revenue, cost, and operational outcomes.
- The portfolio therefore leads with decisions and outcomes, not with technology stacks or responsibility lists.

## Operating Context

- Visitors evaluate the site during candidate or vendor comparison, often from a link in an application, message, or
  referral.
- Primary audiences split across two markets: Brazilian-Portuguese and international English.
- The portfolio is professional evidence; the Farrel.tech business site is a separate future destination.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Stack: Astro, TypeScript, Markdown via Astro Content Collections, CSS.
- Domain: `portfolio.farrel.tech`. `farrel.tech` is reserved for the later business site.
- Languages: Portuguese and English both required. English is the default at the root; Portuguese is served under a
  `/pt` prefix.
- Projects are statically generated from Markdown content collections.
- Contact: a server-backed contact form is anticipated but **not decided** — no provider, endpoint, or host chosen.
  Until then, contact is direct links only.
- Project fields: `title`, `description`, `year`, `role` required; `featured`, `cover`, `tags` optional.
- A case study belongs to exactly one project.
- Unknown outcomes must never be fabricated.

## Brand Commitments

- Name presented as Farrel.
- Contact paths: email, WhatsApp, LinkedIn, GitHub.
- At least one contact path must always remain available.

## Evidence on Hand

- Client and freelance projects.
- Employment work, some of it subject to confidentiality limits.
- Personal and side products that shipped.
- Real measurable outcomes exist for at least some work and may be cited.
- No real project content is in the repository yet: `src/content/projects/sample-project.md` is a placeholder, and the
  home, about, and contact pages carry placeholder copy. Future work must source real content from Farrel rather than
  invent projects, clients, metrics, or testimonials.

## Product Principles

- Work is the primary evidence of competence; self-description is secondary.
- Measurable outcomes outrank descriptions of responsibilities.
- Product and engineering decisions outrank implementation trivia; technology appears only when it explains a
  constraint, decision, or outcome.
- Every claim of capability is backed by work, experience, or an outcome when evidence exists.
- Each fact has one canonical presentation; repetition must earn its place.

## Scope

In scope:

- Product Engineer positioning.
- Selected work.
- Case studies.
- About information relevant to professional evaluation.
- Contact paths.

Out of scope:

- Full résumé replacement.
- Personal blog.
- General-purpose content publishing.
- The Farrel.tech business website.
- Client or lead management.
