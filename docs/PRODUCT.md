# Product

## Problem

- Farrel's engineering work, product judgment, and measurable impact are not presented in one clear public destination.
- Potential employers, clients, and collaborators need to understand what Farrel does and why his work matters without
  relying on a résumé or explanation.

## Solution

- A personal Product Engineer portfolio centered on real work, decisions, and outcomes.
- Projects are presented as evidence of product and engineering capability rather than as technology showcases.

## User

- Engineering leaders evaluating Farrel for Product Engineering roles.
- Product leaders evaluating technical and product judgment.
- Founders and companies looking for freelance or contract Product Engineering work.
- Potential Farrel.tech clients evaluating previous work.

## Value

- Visitors can quickly understand what Farrel does, how he approaches problems, and the outcomes he has produced.
- Farrel can use one public destination to support job opportunities, contract work, and business leads.

## Core Loop

- Visitor understands Farrel's positioning.
- Visitor explores selected work.
- Visitor evaluates a case study.
- Visitor decides whether Farrel is relevant to their problem or opportunity.
- Visitor reaches a contact path.

## Scope

- Product Engineer positioning.
- Selected work.
- Case studies.
- About information relevant to professional evaluation.
- Contact paths.

## Out of Scope

- Full résumé replacement.
- Personal blog.
- General-purpose content publishing.
- Farrel.tech business website.
- Client management or lead management.
