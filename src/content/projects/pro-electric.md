Client project for Pro Electric.

## Problem

Pro Electric relies on manual processes to record and organize information during its daily operation.

A broader custom operations platform could eventually improve scheduling, dispatching, and coordination, but the team is not ready to replace its current workflow with a fully custom system yet.

Before making that investment, the team wants to adopt an established product and understand how well the operation adapts to a more structured digital workflow.

## Approach

Instead of starting with the full operations platform, the project focuses on a smaller problem that can be improved immediately.

The first version is a bot-based MVP designed to reduce manual note-taking and introduce a more structured way of capturing operational information.

This creates value without forcing a large behavioral or technical change on the team.

## Solution

A bot-first workflow that captures and organizes operational notes currently handled manually.

The broader operations platform remains a possible future project after the team has enough experience with digital workflows to understand what should actually be custom-built.

## Outcome

The MVP is currently focused on validating the workflow and reducing manual work.

No broader operational impact is claimed yet.
