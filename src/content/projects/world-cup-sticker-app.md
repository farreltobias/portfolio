Personal product built around the workflow of collecting World Cup stickers.

## Problem

Managing a physical sticker collection becomes increasingly difficult as the collection grows.

Collectors need to know what they already own, what is missing, what they have available for trade, and update that information repeatedly as new stickers are opened or exchanged.

Manual lists and spreadsheets add friction to something that should be quick.

## Approach

The product treats collection management as a repeated workflow rather than a static checklist.

The focus is on reducing the effort required to update the collection and making the information useful for the next action: collecting, searching, or trading.

## Solution

An application for tracking owned, missing, and duplicate stickers.

The product explores faster ways to add collection data, including scanning and structured import workflows, while keeping the collection itself as the source of truth.

## Outcome

The project demonstrates end-to-end product ownership: identifying the user problem, defining the workflow, designing the product model, and implementing the solution.

Usage and business metrics are not claimed until real data is available.
