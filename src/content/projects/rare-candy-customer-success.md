Internal product work at Rare Candy.

## Problem

Customer Success workflows repeatedly required engineering involvement for operational actions that were predictable and recurring.

This created unnecessary interruptions for engineers and slowed down the Customer Success team whenever a technical intervention was needed.

## Approach

The work focused on identifying support requests that did not require engineering judgment and turning those recurring actions into safe internal workflows.

The goal was not simply to automate individual requests, but to reduce the dependency between Customer Success and engineering.

## Solution

Internal tooling that allowed the Customer Success team to perform recurring operational actions without requiring an engineer for each request.

The product exposed the necessary workflows while preserving the constraints required by the underlying systems.

## Outcome

Engineering support demand associated with these workflows dropped from roughly four requests per day to around one or two per week.

The result was less interruption for engineering and greater operational autonomy for Customer Success.
