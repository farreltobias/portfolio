# PORT-002 - Home Content

## Target

- Home page content and information hierarchy.

## Change

- Replace placeholder home content with real portfolio messaging aligned with Farrel's Product Engineer positioning.
- Define the minimum content required for a visitor to understand relevance, evaluate selected work, and reach a contact path.

## Constraints

- English is the default language.
- Portuguese content is handled separately under `/pt`.
- Lead with work, decisions, and outcomes rather than résumé-style responsibilities or technology stacks.
- Position Farrel as a Product Engineer who owns product decisions end to end and frames technical decisions by business impact.
- Use only verified work, experience, metrics, clients, and outcomes.
- Do not invent testimonials, projects, metrics, or claims.
- Keep the Home focused on rapid evaluation by visitors arriving from applications, referrals, outreach, or vendor comparison.
- Do not make page-level visual or layout decisions in this task.

## Done

- Hero messaging clearly communicates who Farrel is and the type of problems he solves.
- Supporting copy communicates the Product Engineer positioning without relying on a technology list.
- A selected work section has real content requirements defined.
- A concise professional context section is defined only if it adds information not already communicated by the work.
- A primary contact CTA is defined.
- Home copy contains no placeholder claims or fabricated evidence.
- Content provides enough information for the next task to make presentation decisions without inventing product requirements.
